# Knowledge retrieval evaluation

Generated by `python -m evals.retrieval_eval --write`. Do not edit by hand.

- Golden set: 40 customer questions over 5 synthetic documents (9,992 characters) in `evals/retrieval_golden.json`.
- Metric: **answer-in-context recall** — the share of questions whose answer sentence is present, verbatim, in the text selected for the model. It is checked with the same function that verifies citations in production.
- Budget: the share of the whole knowledge base that fits in the model context. Production has a fixed 22,000-character budget, so a smaller share means a larger knowledge base.

## Limits of this evaluation

- The documents and questions are synthetic and written for this test; real knowledge bases will differ.
- BM25 matches words, not meaning. Questions phrased with entirely different vocabulary than the source can be missed; semantic (embedding) retrieval is the natural next step and can be measured with this same set.
- This measures retrieval only. Whether the model then answers correctly is covered by the in-app training simulator.
