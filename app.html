Your AI uses Groq to write and check replies. It starts with automatic sending off. You choose which mailboxes, if any, may reply on their own.

When your AI works on a reply, it can share the email’s subject and text, your company facts, and text from saved documents with Groq. It does not share email attachments or the original document files. Groq says it does not keep these inputs and replies by default, but may keep them for up to 30 days for reliability or abuse investigations.
